The Destruction of Plastic app is an application designed to make student's lives easier. It does this by taking a student's name, identification number, and infromation held on the Barcode featured on the back of the card and stores the data. It does this through the 
"ai_barcode_scanner: ^8.2.0" created by Mohesu. This sensor utilizes the camera to scan the barcode on the back of an ID card, and generate the QR or Barcode needed for the app. The app works by opening the camera to run the card scanner, scan the card, and store the card until needed.
When needed the student can press on the arrow button located on the card profile to retrieve the card's Barcode data. This will also run a QR or Barcode creater program, that will allow the code to be displayed on the screen and scanned. We have also implemented the ability to edit you profile info,
in case something is spelled wrong or there was a misinput when entering the ID number. The reason we built this app is that students may lose their ID card and not be able to get food at the cafeteria or scan into to specific places on their campus which uses a QR or Barcode scanner. 
By adding their card to this app, the student's data is saved locally allowing them to use their barcode even when they are not connected to wifi. 
However, we plan to add accounts and RFID scanners to allow students to access their dorms, and hold their information securely. The purpose of the accounts will be to add a layer of security to prevent other individuals from accessing or storring sensitive information that
belongs to the student.
